Irovi is a wellness diary that helps you keep track of iron-rich foods and your blood test results. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter.

**What we collect**
Nothing. Irovi does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. The app has no account, no servers, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it contains no third-party SDKs.

**What stays on your iPhone**
Everything you enter (your daily checklist, the blood test results you type in, notes, medicines you add, and your settings) is stored only on your device, inside the app's own storage. We cannot see it.

**Reminders**
Reminders are local notifications created on your device. They are scheduled only if you turn them on, and their text does not include your health values.

**Sharing**
Your data leaves your device only if you choose to export it (as a CSV file) and share it yourself, for example by email or AirDrop.

**Deleting your data**
You can delete all data in Settings → Delete all data, or by deleting the app.

**Children**
Irovi is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them.

**Health information**
Irovi is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Talk to a doctor about your health.

**Changes**
If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
